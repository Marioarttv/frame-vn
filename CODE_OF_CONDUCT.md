# Community conduct

Frame welcomes people with different backgrounds, skills, and levels of experience. Help others participate by being respectful, patient, and specific.

- Discuss ideas and behavior without personal attacks, harassment, discriminatory language, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's personal details, private media, or credentials.
- Give constructive feedback, explain decisions, and acknowledge corrections.
- Keep project spaces focused on creating and maintaining the software and its documentation.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close discussions, or restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Reports should include the relevant interaction and its context. To request a private reporting channel, contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile without posting sensitive details publicly. Security vulnerabilities use the separate process in SECURITY.md.

These expectations apply to this repository's issues, pull requests, and other project-managed community spaces. Maintainer availability is limited; the project does not promise continuous moderation or fixed response times.
